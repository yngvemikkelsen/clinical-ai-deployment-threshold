Multimedia Appendix 1 - coding definitions
=========================================

Scope of these codings
  Every coding in this frame is MECHANISTIC: it records an
  implementation characteristic expected to correspond to the
  response-defined subgroup of the decision model, not a measured
  response to the intervention. No system in the frame was evaluated
  under the intervention. The counts here therefore support proxy-based
  partial identification under stated classification assumptions; they
  do not bound response-defined prevalence, which is unidentified
  without such an assumption.

Mechanistic affected criterion
  A system is coded as meeting the hypothesised affected mechanism
  only where BOTH hold:
   (a) the representation actually used for retrieval can be
       established from the primary article, and
   (b) that representation was not shown to have been calibrated by
       the model's training objective.
  Systems whose extraction step cannot be established from the primary
  article are coded UNKNOWN, not unaffected. One of 55 systems meets
  this mechanistic criterion. No system in the frame was evaluated
  under the intervention, so the count does not bound response-defined
  prevalence. It enters the analysis only under explicit
  classification assumptions.

Reported pooling
  None of the 55 systems reports a retrieval pooling strategy as such.
  One (A50) reports enough implementation detail elsewhere in its
  methods to infer the extraction step; the inference, its basis, and
  the primary-source location are given in their own columns.

Coding stability
  A50 is Tier 2 in the core, expanded and adversarial frames. The
  source workbook additionally flagged it ambiguous under the ORIGINAL
  rationale (no similarity-tuned checkpoint identified), which the
  manuscript establishes is invalid: citing the base-model paper does
  not establish the absence of a similarity-tuned checkpoint. Under
  the extracted-representation basis the coding is not ambiguous.
  Both the
  superseded rationale and the current basis are recorded so the
  change is auditable.
