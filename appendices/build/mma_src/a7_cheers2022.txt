Item 4. Reported as a negative in Strengths and Limitations: no health economic analysis plan was prespecified or registered. The model, its parameters, and the analysis code are archived and publicly available, which supports reproducibility but does not substitute for a prespecified plan.

Item 5. The unit of analysis is a deployed retrieval system rather than a patient cohort. Population characteristics are described at the level of the health system whose activity determines deployment scale, and at the level of the document corpora used to measure the technical effect. No patient-level characteristics are used in the decision model.

Item 19. Distributional effects across individuals were not modelled. The analysis concerns whether an intervention should be deployed across a system, not how its consequences distribute across patient groups. Stated as a limitation.

Item 21. Reported as a negative in Strengths and Limitations: no patient, public, or stakeholder engagement was undertaken. The study is a secondary analysis of published system descriptions and de-identified document corpora, with no primary data collection.
